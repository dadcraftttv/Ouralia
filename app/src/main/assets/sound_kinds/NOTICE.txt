Sound kinds (step 4c; in every build since Nov 5, ARM phones only; docs/design/sound-kinds.md)

yamnet.tflite: YAMNet, Google (tensorflow/models, research/audioset/yamnet), the "classification-tflite" v1 model
from Kaggle (google/yamnet). Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0:
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
Changed by Ouralia (Nov 6, accuracy step 4d): its 1024-value embedding (layer28/reduce_mean) is added as a second
output by tools/sound_kinds/embed_model.py; the network and its scores are unchanged.

The class names inside it come from the AudioSet ontology, Google, licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), used here under GPLv3 by CC's one-way
compatibility declaration. kinds.txt groups those classes into Ouralia's kinds (tools/sound_kinds/bench.py).

The model runs on LiteRT (com.google.ai.edge.litert:litert 1.4.2, Google), also licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 (the full text is under "Oboe and libpd (Apache 2.0)" in this list).

layer.bin (Hear Far Sounds, accuracy step 4d; docs/design/sound-kinds.md "Accuracy step 4")

A small layer on YAMNet's embedding, trained by tools/sound_kinds/step4_train.py on public recordings:
61 room tones from Freesound (freesound.org) and 43 sound clips from Wikimedia Commons
and Freesound (by way of the ESC-50 set), each kept under its own licence: CC0, public domain or CC BY.
The layer holds no audio, only numbers learned from it. The clips were cut, levelled, mixed and given room
echo for training. The CC BY recordings, with their creators:
